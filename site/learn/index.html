What is TikiDeco?

TikiDeco explores how hospitality loyalty rules and project reports could become easier for people to check. Today you can explore a public test-network prototype and a demonstration using fictional data.

Sepolia prototype only. TIDE is not offered for sale, has no stated monetary value, and is not deployed on mainnet. Independent audit not started.

Understand the idea

The problem
People need to understand participation rules, availability and who makes decisions before using a loyalty program.
The experiment
TikiDeco tests public rules, read-only eligibility checks and transparent reporting. Hospitality use remains conceptual.
What TIDE means
TIDE is the project's test token on Ethereum Sepolia. Sepolia is a testing network, separate from Ethereum mainnet.
